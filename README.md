Mental Health Risk Screening Chatbot using Generative AI

Problem Statement:
Mental health concerns such as stress, anxiety, and depressive symptoms are increasingly prevalent, yet early identification remains limited due to stigma, lack of awareness, and absence of accessible screening tools. Many individuals do not have an easy, private, and non-judgmental way to reflect on their emotional well-being or recognize early signs of mental distress.
The challenge addressed in this project is to provide an accessible and ethically safe conversational system that can help users perform early mental health risk screening and receive supportive guidance without attempting medical diagnosis or treatment.

Solution Overview:
This project proposes a Generative AI–based conversational chatbot designed to perform structured mental health risk screening through natural language interaction.
The chatbot will:
Engage users in a conversational manner
Administer standardized mental health screening questions
Analyze responses using Large Language Models (LLMs)
Retrieve relevant support content from a knowledge base using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Generate empathetic, non-diagnostic responses using agent-based reasoning
The system is designed with safety and ethical constraints to ensure it functions as a support and awareness tool, not as a diagnostic or clinical system.
